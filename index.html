Questions, answered

Do you really make videos without shooting?

For most projects, yes. We start from your real photos and use AI tools to add motion, camera moves and scenes. If you want real footage in the mix, we can combine both.

Will it look like my real place or product?

That’s our main focus. We start from your photos, you approve a preview before the final render, and we check details like your logo, signs and layout.

What do you need from us?

5 to 10 clear photos, your logo, and a few lines about what you want to say. Good light matters more than a fancy camera.

How long does a project take?

A reel takes about 5 to 7 working days, and a brand film takes 10 to 15, depending on how fast you send feedback.

How many revisions do we get?

Two rounds are included with every deliverable. Extra rounds are charged at a small fixed fee.

Do you also post and manage our social media?

Yes. Our monthly plans cover the content calendar, reels, captions and posting. Ad spend on Instagram or Google is paid separately.

How do payments work?

50% in advance to start, and the rest on delivery. Monthly plans are paid in advance.
